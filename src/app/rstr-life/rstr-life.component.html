RSTR Life

Restore the day you meant to live.

A quieter rhythm for work, rest, and the hours that actually belong to you.

The rhythm

Life first. Work finds its place.

RSTR is short for restore. The idea is simple: put yourself at the center of the schedule, then let work orbit around the hours that keep you whole.

A What restores you
B What pays the bills
Either can spill into the other — you decide which one leads.

The practice

One day. Clear hours. Honest attention.

Map the day in living blocks — not task lists. See where your time went, protect the hours that rebuild you, and treat work as one client among many.

  • Morning seal Name the three hours that restore you before the inbox opens.
  • Living columns Track A-life and B-work side by side so neither becomes invisible.
  • Evening return Close the day with a short note — what returned energy, what took it.
